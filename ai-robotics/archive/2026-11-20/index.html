The Geometry of Lies: Knowledge and Persona Directions for Detecting Deception in Large Language Models

The authors build label-free persona and knowledge directions in LLM activations to detect lies, including lies where a false denial reads like an honest one. They report that the persona direction often beats single-dataset probes but fails on Qwen, and that the combined directions outrank a supervised probe at equal depth.

PhyDEX: A Benchmark for Physical Reasoning in Vision-Language-Action Models via Dynamic Extrinsic Dexterity

PhyDEX is a throwing benchmark of 35K automatically generated trajectories in which a robot must infer mass, center of mass and friction to land objects outside its reach. The authors report that three evaluated VLA models succeed under 3% of the time, with success roughly doubling when true physical properties are provided.

Probing the "Conscious" Mind: Selective Access and Global Workspace in Vision-Language Models

Drawing on global workspace theory, the authors propose MC-Lens, a multimodal Jacobian lens that maps vision encoder and language decoder states into a shared vocabulary space and supports concept interventions. They report that concept information is selectively accessible as tasks demand and that counterfactual edits show it feeds into downstream reasoning.

JARVIS: An Agent That Designs, Builds, and Tests Circuit Boards on Its Own

JARVIS is an embodied agent that designs, prints, assembles and tests circuit boards, rehearsing each step in a scanned digital twin before using materials. In a 60 Hz notch filter demonstration, the authors report a first run completed without human intervention in 2 h 39 min, with the rehearsal catching five issues beforehand.

LLM GerryMandering: Separating Interactions from Additive Effects in LLM Audits

The authors show that a common criterion for flagging intersectional bias produces many false positives on synthetic data lacking any interaction, and propose ADDIS, which tests deviations from an additive model. Auditing 13 open language models on 10,000 synthetic Indian personas, they report ADDIS flagging fewer subgroups than the standard criterion.

AgentRoboCritic: Understanding the Capability of Frontier Agents on Direct Robot Control

AgentRoboCritic has a policy agent state a local intent with each low-level robot action, letting a critic judge outcomes and failure modes across 12 manipulation benchmarks. The authors report strengths in recovery and semantic grounding, weaknesses in grasping and alignment, and release a dataset plus a fine-tuned open critic.

Awaken-HI: Scaling Humanoid Intelligence with Whole-Body Human Experience

The authors collect roughly 50,000 hours of egocentric stereo video paired with whole-body human motion and train Awaken-HI, a humanoid VLA model that jointly predicts human motion and retargeted robot actions. They report a scaling trend with more human data and a 93.75% mean success rate over eight real tasks.

Self-Replicating Language Models

Framing the question of whether language models could count as Darwinian life, the authors test ways a model might reproduce itself, such as emitting a training genome, mutating its weights, or teaching offspring. They report that small models can replicate and adapt under selection, while large-model replication by teaching remains infeasible for now.

Teaching Vision-Language-Action Models to Stop Safely

The authors report that vision-language-action policies trained to refuse a task from the start may still finish it, or drop held objects, when they take control midway through execution. They propose State-Aware Fallback Imitation, which labels prohibited demonstrations with stationary arm and steady gripper targets and trains these jointly with retained tasks, reporting 1.3% target success after takeover on LIBERO with no physical incidents.
