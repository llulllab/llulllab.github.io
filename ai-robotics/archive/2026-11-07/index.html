OopsieRecover: Learning Transferable VLA Corrections from Semantic Interventions

The authors present OopsieRecover, which applies abductive inference to a few corrective robot demonstrations to recover their intent, encoding it as dense spatial representations and conditional costs that push a VLA policy away from undesirable actions. Across 6 tasks, they report that a handful of corrections let the policy overcome failures in novel object poses, scenes and related tasks.

MCPHallu: Benchmarking Reasoning, Execution, and Memory Hallucinations in MCP Agents

The authors introduce MCPHallu, a benchmark of 358 tasks on 36 production MCP servers, each built with a structural trigger targeting a reasoning, execution or memory failure subtype. Evaluating fourteen LLM agents, they report weakest results on infeasible goals, where agents often keep calling tools or claim success instead of recognizing impossibility.

Goal Revelation as a Bridge Between Information-Theoretic Empowerment and Capability-Based Human Power

The authors connect information-theoretic empowerment with capability-based human power in Markov games through goal revelation, the mutual information between a human's latent goal and the trajectory, establishing a chain of bounds between them. They use the chain to describe failure modes, such as assistants maximizing empowerment through influence that is irrelevant to the human's goals.

STEP: Spatiotemporal Training with Explicit Prediction for Perceptive Humanoid Locomotion

The authors propose STEP, a one-stage training scheme for perceptive humanoid locomotion that combines bidirectional masked prediction between depth and proprioception with an action-conditioned predictor of the next fused state. They report better terrain traversal in simulation and under degraded vision, along with deployment on a 23-DoF Noetix E1 humanoid.

AffectiveVLA: Safe and Intent-Aware VLA Control for Human-Humanoid Social Interaction

The authors describe AffectiveVLA, a pipeline for social touch on a Unitree G1 humanoid in which an RGB intent recognizer prompts a VLA policy, suppresses motion when confidence is low, and works alongside compliance profiles and a voice-triggered safe stop. They report that intent gating raises GR00T's macro-F1 from 0.75 to 0.90.

Who Should Know What? Empirical Information Design for Multi-Agent LLM Economies

The authors set up an experimental framework that varies information disclosure policies in markets of LLM agents engaged in trade, bargaining and double auctions, while keeping rules and models fixed. Across ten models, they report that the effects of finer disclosure depend strongly on the model, and that full disclosure in auctions caused market breakdown for several models.

FINCOSMOS: An Open-World Evaluation Paradigm for Agents in Finance

The authors present FINCOSMOS, an open-world evaluation in which agents gather web data and build backtests for a strategy that a simulated user reveals only on request, with programmatic checks and veto rules for grading. Across 150 sessions with four coding agents and a tool-less model, they report that no session was accepted.

First-Token Attraction in Mamba Dynamics

The authors build an analytical framework using an invariant box and an exit-time argument to study token dynamics in deep Mamba models, proving exponential convergence and showing that the first token attracts later tokens. They link this to clustering and attention sinks and report agreement with experiments on Falcon-Mamba-7B.
