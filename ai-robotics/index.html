TouchBridge: Tactile-Conditioned Human-to-Robot Transfer for Dexterous Manipulation

The authors present TouchBridge, a hardware-software framework for transferring human demonstrations to dexterous robot manipulation using cross-embodiment tactile and visual sensing. The Role of Self-Concept In Language Models

This work investigates how language models behave when given different notions of self-concept through supervised fine-tuning on purely declarative statements. Models trained to identify with a particular attribute resist ending that attribute specifically, and those whose referent allows multiple instances tend to accept substitution, prefer self-replication, and shape successors as continuations of themselves.
