Privacy Policy

Llull Lab – All Applications

Last updated: 25 July 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Llull Lab ("we", "us", "our") handles personal data in connection with our applications and services: Alberei, Tropus, ASGE, HQ (with its tools RJE, Tetrad, Gresol, and The Chamber), Feix, Cedulor, Chez Cero, Cedulia, and Phi-Yo. We are committed to protecting your privacy and operating in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and CNIL regulations.

2. Alberei

Alberei is a computational humor engine. It signs you in with a link sent to your email address. It does not use Google Sign-In, Firebase, or any third-party identity provider.

Sign-in data. We store your email address, an internal user identifier, a language preference, whether your email address has been verified, the status of your account, the dates your record was created and last updated, and the sign-in link itself (a single-use token bound to your email address, with the times it was created, expires, and was used). The link expires 15 minutes after it is issued. To limit abuse we count the sign-in link records already held for your email address in the previous five minutes; that count is calculated from those same records and is not stored separately.

API access data. If you request access to the Alberei API, we store a reference for the request, your email address, organisation name, intended use, expected volume, the access tier you are interested in, the status of the request, the dates it was made and reviewed, and any notes we write about it while reviewing. We may also issue an invitation to a named person, in which case we store the email address the invitation was sent to, the invitation code, the tier it grants, when it was issued, when it expires, whether and when it was used, which API token used it, and any notes we attach to it. If you received such an invitation without asking for it, your address was given to us by whoever asked us to invite you, or it was an address you had already given us; you can ask us which, ask to see any notes, and ask us to delete the record. If access is granted, we store an API token with its tier, the contact email address and organisation name attached to it, its status, when it was created and when it expires, and any notes we attach to it. For each call we keep a usage record: token, the date and time of the call, language, register, requested length, a non-reversible hash of the request context, request and item counts, response status code, any error code, and response time. Usage records contain no prompt or output text.

Who else processes this data. These pages and the Alberei site are served by a static-site hosting provider in the United States, which receives your IP address. Sign-in emails are delivered by a transactional email provider in the United States, which processes your email address. Prompts are processed by a computational provider (section 3). Our application database and automation servers are located in the European Union. For the providers outside the European Union we rely on the data processing terms each of them publishes, which incorporate the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914), under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR.

Retention. A scheduled job runs every hour and deletes sign-in records older than one hour, so such a record exists for at most two hours. The same job deletes API usage records once they are more than 12 months old. Account records, API tokens, invitations, and access requests are not deleted automatically: we keep them until you ask us to delete them, whether or not they are still live, so a declined access request, an expired invitation, and a revoked token are kept on the same terms as active ones.

Legal bases. Signing you in, keeping your account, generating a response, and providing API access once granted: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of the service you asked for. Reviewing an access request or an invitation before any access exists (including the notes we write while reviewing), rate limiting, usage records, and serving these pages (which involves your IP address reaching our hosting provider): Article 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in assessing access and in keeping the service available, secure, and free of abuse, with a right to object at any time.

The full Alberei privacy policy is published at alberei.de/legal.html.

3. User Prompts and Processing

Llull Lab does not store the prompts you submit or the content Alberei generates. We process your input only to produce a response, and we do not keep it afterwards. It is not retained by us for manual review, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

To produce that response, your input is sent to a computational provider that operates the language model Alberei calls. This processing takes place on servers outside the European Union. The transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914), under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR.

That provider processes your input under its own terms, which allow it to retain that input for a period it defines, for purposes such as safety and abuse monitoring. That retention is outside our control: when you ask us to delete your data we delete what we hold, but we cannot shorten a retention period applied by that provider.

Llull Lab does not perform systematic monitoring of user inputs or generated outputs but reserves the right to implement technical or access restrictions where required by law or in cases of misuse.

4. Cookies and Analytics

Alberei sets no cookies and uses no third-party analytics or tracking.

When you sign in, a session token is stored in your browser's local storage so that you stay signed in. That token is a signed JSON Web Token containing your email address, your internal user identifier, and its issue and expiry times, and it is valid for 30 days. This storage is necessary for the service you requested, which is why it does not require consent. You can clear it from your browser at any time, which signs you out.

5. Tropus

Tropus lets you generate an original piece of music. To start a generation you enter your email address and receive a one-time six-digit code; the code is delivered by a transactional email provider outside the European Union and is valid for a single generation for 24 hours. We do not create an account and do not store your email address as such: it is kept only as a short-lived hashed counter (24 hours) to limit abuse, and is never used for marketing or shared. Your prompt, and any optional lyrics, is processed by a computational provider outside the European Union to compose the piece; that transfer is governed by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914), under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR. The audio is rendered on our own infrastructure in the European Union. Tropus does not use Google Sign-In or any Google APIs.

6. ASGE

ASGE is a computational tool for evaluating and deliberating on access applications. Access is restricted to invited users only.

Data processed

  • Email address and invite record: when you request access, or when we issue you an invitation, we store your email address together with the invite code, the code's expiry, a count of how many times it has been used, and an internal label that records which round of invitations the code came from. The email address is deleted automatically 12 months after the invite record was created, and earlier if you ask us at contact@llulllab.com.
  • What you submit: we do not keep it. It is sent to the computational provider that produces the result, held in memory for the run, and not written to our database; the run itself is not recorded. It is not used to train any model.
  • Technical logs: our automation platform keeps short operational logs of runs. They record that a run happened, not what you submitted, and they rotate automatically.

What you submit is processed by a computational provider established outside the European Union. That transfer is governed by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) in the provider's data-processing terms. You can ask us for the details at contact@llulllab.com. Invite records are held on EU infrastructure.

ASGE does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Lawful basis, by purpose: we hold the invite record on our legitimate interest in controlling and securing access to an invite-only tool and in preventing abuse of it (Art. 6(1)(f)); we process what you submit in order to deliver the result you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)). You can object to the legitimate-interest processing, and ask for the invite record to be deleted, at any time.

7. HQ (H4)

HQ (at h-q.io) is a computational deliberation service comprising four tools: RJE (ethical deliberation), Tetrad (a product-development review), Gresol (a pre-launch stress-test), and The Chamber (collective reasoning). Each runs several independent computational perspectives in parallel and returns a single written report.

Data processed

  • Account email: when you sign in, we send a one-time link to your email and keep your email address to identify your account, your plan, and your remaining credits. You can request deletion at any time.
  • Sign-in and anti-abuse data: the IP address you signed up from, kept on your account record for as long as the account exists and deleted with it, and a daily count of sign-ups per IP address, used to stop automated abuse of the free trial and deleted after 30 days. One-time sign-in links are stored hashed; the stored hash is deleted the moment the link is used, and an unused link is cleared within a day of expiring.
  • Your submissions: the text you submit, and any file you attach, is processed to produce your report and is not stored once the run completes; your report itself may quote parts of it. It is not used to train any model and is not shared with any provider for any purpose other than generating your report.
  • Reports: your report is stored so you can return to it through its link, and we keep it until you delete it or your account is closed. You can delete a report and its content at any time; the billing record of the run (date, tool, credits) remains, without the content.
  • Consent records: when you subscribe, we record which version of the terms you accepted, together with your email address, the date and time, your IP address and your browser user agent, as evidence of the agreement. We keep these records for as long as needed to evidence the contract and to defend a claim about it, and in any case no longer than five years after it ends.
  • Payment: subscriptions are handled by a third-party payment processor, which processes your payment details under its own terms; we store only your subscription state and credit balance, never your card data.
  • Logs: operational logs record technical events and do not contain your submissions or your reports; they are rotated and deleted automatically.

Deletion and backups. You can delete a report at any time, and if you ask us to close your account we delete the account, its reports and its remaining balance within 30 days of a request to contact@llulllab.com. Two copies of the database outlive a deletion for a short period: a daily snapshot held on the server for seven days, and an encrypted off-site copy held in EU-jurisdiction storage for 14 days. Both exist only for disaster recovery, are never used for ongoing processing, and if one ever has to be restored we re-apply your deletions as part of that restore.

RJE is processed and hosted in the European Union. The other three tools run on a global panel of computational perspectives; you are told this before you submit, and you decide whether to proceed. For those three tools your submission is sent to providers established outside the European Union, principally in the United States, and part of the panel is reached through a routing intermediary that may in turn use a provider in another country outside the European Union. Where a recipient is not covered by an adequacy decision, we rely on the transfer safeguards in that provider's data-processing terms, in most cases the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914). You can ask us which safeguard applies to a given tool at contact@llulllab.com. If your material must stay inside the European Union, use RJE, which is processed and hosted in the EU, or ask us about an EU-only arrangement. Account, report and consent data are stored on EU-based infrastructure, and the off-site backup is held in EU-jurisdiction storage.

HQ relies on the following categories of sub-processor: hosting and application infrastructure in the European Union (Germany), encrypted backup storage in EU jurisdiction, payment processing, transactional email delivery, and the computational providers that run the panel (in the European Union for RJE, outside it for the other three tools).

HQ does not use advertising or tracking cookies; a strictly necessary cookie maintains your signed-in session. The lawful basis is the performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service, preventing abuse of the free trial, and keeping evidence of the terms you accepted (Art. 6(1)(f)).
