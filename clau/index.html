Private by designConfidentiel par conception

No email, no phone, no account. The text you work on is sent to an EU-hosted language model to generate Clau's replies: it is processed in the EU to answer you and is not saved in our database. If you choose to keep your work to continue later, it is encrypted in your browser under a key only you hold, so we cannot read that saved copy. We keep only minimal technical logs to prevent abuse. Your work is processed and stored in the EU.Ni e-mail, ni telephone, ni compte.
