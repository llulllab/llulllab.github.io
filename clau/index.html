Doctoral funding radar · 3 November 2026

Doctoral and Early-Career Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

A strong funding plan starts with matching the instrument to your career stage. The calls below run from structured doctoral training, through postdoctoral fellowships, to grants for consolidating your own research team. Read each description for the outcomes it expects, then work backwards from the deadline to plan realistic preparation time.

Of the five calls below, three are applied for by institutions or consortia, with individual positions recruited after the grant is awarded, and two are applied for directly by the individual researcher, together with a host organisation.

ERC Consolidator Grant

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open Closes on 12 January 2027 70 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

The Consolidator Grant is aimed at Principal Investigators who are consolidating their own independent research team or programme. For a researcher a few years past the doctorate, it is a way to turn a body of work into a sustained programme with its own people and resources. The description also allows a duly justified request for additional funding when the proposed work requires further costs, which matters if your project depends on substantial equipment or infrastructure.

Preparation step. Draft a two page outline of the research programme you want to consolidate, setting out the team roles it needs and any additional costs you would have to justify. Then open a conversation with a prospective host organisation and check every eligibility condition on the official topic page before you commit further time.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

MSCA COFUND (doctoral programmes)

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 154 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

This call supports doctoral programmes that organisations design and co-finance, so the proposal is prepared at institutional level rather than by candidates. Doctoral candidates benefit later, once a funded programme opens its positions through its own vacancy calls. The expected outcomes describe a broader skill set for those candidates, international and interdisciplinary experience, and stronger communication with peers and the general public.

Preparation step

List the universities and graduate schools in your field that run or are building co-funded doctoral programmes, and follow their announcements so you see the positions they advertise if they are funded. In the meantime, build evidence of public communication or outreach, which the description names as an expected outcome for supported candidates.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

MSCA COFUND (postdoctoral programmes)

MSCA Choose Europe for Science 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 154 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Choose Europe for Science funds postdoctoral programmes run by participating organisations, which later open researcher positions through their own selection rounds. For researchers, the described outcomes focus on concrete career prospects, greater career stability and growing autonomy. The call also expects organisations to align recruitment and career progression with the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment, which shapes how future candidates will be assessed.

Preparation step

Read the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment, since the description names both as reference points for recruitment and progression. Then follow the postdoctoral programmes at organisations you would like to join, and watch for the positions they open once their programmes are funded.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-02-01

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027

Opens on 7 April 2027 Closes on 8 September 2027 309 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowships let a researcher propose their own project together with a host organisation. The expected outcomes centre on widening your research and professional competences, broadening your career options inside and outside universities, and building networks with scientific peers and the public. Participating organisations are expected to offer quality supervision and working conditions in line with the European Charter for Researchers, so your choice of host carries real weight.

Preparation step

Identify one or two prospective supervisors and contact them early to discuss how their group would support your training and supervision. Before investing serious drafting time, verify the mobility rule and all other eligibility conditions on the official topic page.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2027

Opens on 26 May 2027 Closes on 23 November 2027 385 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Doctoral Networks are proposed by groups of participating organisations working together, and the doctoral positions they fund are advertised only after a network is awarded. For prospective candidates, the outcomes describe new research competences, better prospects across sectors and, where relevant, knowledge that turns ideas into products and services. The networks are also expected to deepen cooperation between disciplines and sectors, so the training typically extends beyond a single lab.

Preparation step

Follow research groups and companies in your field that take part in Doctoral Networks, and set up alerts so you catch the doctoral vacancies they publish once a network is funded. Use the waiting period to gain experience that crosses disciplines or sectors, such as a collaboration with an industry partner.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-DN-01-01

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 21 October 2026

Interactive publication featuring over 100 success stories and testimonials

The Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions have released an interactive publication that brings together more than 100 success stories and testimonials from the programme. Readers can browse by country or by institution to find researchers and projects connected to their own background. For doctoral and early-career researchers weighing an application, it offers concrete examples of how others have built careers through the programme.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 20 October 2026

Science without borders: the lasting legacy of Marie Skłodowska-Curie

Marking 30 years of the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions in 2026, this article traces the programme's growth from about 800 supported researchers per year to around 8,000, with more than 150,000 participants since 1996, including 25 Nobel laureates. It also notes that companies make up almost half of participating organisations, many of them small and medium-sized enterprises. For early-career researchers, this points to substantial opportunities for international mobility and for careers that bridge academia and industry.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 6 October 2026

Nobel Prizes in Medicine and Physics awarded to former MSCA fellow and supervisor

Two 2026 Nobel laureates have ties to the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions: Georg Nagel, who shares the Medicine prize for work on light-gated ion channels and optogenetics, held a fellowship at the University of Naples Federico II around 1999 and 2000, and Francis Halzen, awarded the Physics prize for his role in the IceCube Neutrino Observatory, supervised the programme-funded ASTRONU project on neutrino astronomy. The announcement shows how fellowships and training networks can sit within research paths that later lead to major scientific recognition.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 30 September 2026

Over €210 million boost for the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions to support more researchers in Europe

The European Commission has announced more than €210 million in extra funding for the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, presented at the programme's 30th anniversary event as part of its Choose Europe approach, with the increase going to Postdoctoral Fellowships and Doctoral Networks. The top-up responds to record demand: the 2026 Postdoctoral Fellowships call attracted 21,627 applications, the highest number for any action in over 40 years of EU framework programmes. With more than €1.3 billion foreseen for 2027 under the final Horizon Europe work programme, more funded places should open for doctoral and postdoctoral researchers, although competition is likely to remain strong.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 25 September 2026

Cancer research in the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions

This document presents cancer research supported by the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, a programme with a budget of EUR 6.6 billion for 2021 to 2027 that funds training, skills development and mobility for researchers at every career stage. Because the programme works bottom-up and selects projects on scientific excellence, early-career researchers in oncology and related fields can propose their own topics without needing to match a predefined theme.
