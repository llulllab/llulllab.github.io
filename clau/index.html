Doctoral funding radar · 4 November 2026

Doctoral and Early-Career Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

Planning a funding application usually means looking further ahead than feels comfortable, because the calls that shape doctoral and postdoctoral careers run on long cycles. The calls below cover structured doctoral training, postdoctoral career programmes and grants that support a researcher's own line of work under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions and the ERC. Reading them side by side can help you decide where your energy is best spent over the coming year.

Of the five calls below, three are applied for by institutions or consortia, with individual positions recruited after the grant is awarded, and two are applied for directly by the individual researcher, together with a host organisation.

ERC Consolidator Grant

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open Closes on 12 January 2027 69 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

The Consolidator Grant funds Principal Investigators who are consolidating their own independent research team or programme. The call expects evidence that you already work independently, so your proposal needs to present a research agenda that is clearly yours. Additional funding can be requested for further costs, but the call states that any such request must be justified in the proposal.

Preparation step. Draft a two-page outline of the research programme you want to consolidate, setting out what your team will do that you could not do alone. Then check every eligibility condition on the official topic page before committing to a full proposal.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

MSCA COFUND (doctoral programmes)

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 153 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

This Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND call is an opportunity for organisations that run doctoral programmes, not for individual candidates. Its value to you comes later, once funded programmes open their positions. The description promises doctoral candidates a broader skill set, wider career options inside and outside universities, and experience that crosses countries, sectors and disciplines.

Preparation step

Make a list of graduate schools and doctoral programmes in your field that have run COFUND schemes before, and follow their news and vacancy pages. Positions are advertised separately after a grant is awarded, often a year or more later, so use the interval to strengthen your research profile and the materials you will need when calls open.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

MSCA COFUND (postdoctoral programmes)

MSCA Choose Europe for Science 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 153 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Choose Europe for Science is a postdoctoral COFUND call under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions. Organisations compete for funding to run postdoctoral programmes, and researchers join those programmes later through their own open calls. The description puts concrete career prospects and greater career stability first, alongside skills that build research autonomy. Participating organisations are expected to align recruitment and progression practices with the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment.

Preparation step

Identify the universities and research centres in your area that are likely to run programmes under this call, and keep an eye out for the postdoctoral positions they advertise once funded. Because the call emphasises reformed research assessment, prepare a profile that presents the full range of your contributions rather than publications alone.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-02-01

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027

Opens on 7 April 2027 Closes on 8 September 2027 308 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

Postdoctoral Fellowships under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions fund a research project you design yourself, carried out with a host organisation. The description emphasises new skills and experience that can open doors in many career directions, as well as better networking and public communication. On the host side, it stresses working conditions in line with the European Charter for Researchers and good-quality training and supervision. That makes the choice of supervisor and host environment part of the substance of your proposal.

Preparation step

Shortlist two or three prospective supervisors whose groups complement your expertise, and contact them early with a short project sketch. Before you invest serious time, verify the mobility rule and all other eligibility conditions on the official topic page.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2027

Opens on 26 May 2027 Closes on 23 November 2027 384 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Doctoral Networks are proposed by partnerships of organisations, which then fund and train doctoral candidates jointly. You cannot be part of this round's proposal; the positions come later through separate vacancy calls. What sets the description apart is its attention to turning ideas into products and services where relevant, and to knowledge transfer between sectors and disciplines. Doctoral candidates in these networks can expect training that reaches beyond a single lab.

Preparation step

Follow research groups and industry partners in your field that have taken part in Doctoral Networks, for example through their project websites and mailing lists. Watch for the doctoral vacancies they post once funded, and in the meantime build evidence of interest in work across sectors, such as collaborations, internships or applied projects.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-DN-01-01

European Research Council · 23 October 2026

Starting Grants 2027 applications: Facts and figures

Preliminary figures for the ERC Starting Grants 2027 call, which closed in autumn 2026, show 7 710 submissions, a rise of 60.4% on the previous round and the third consecutive year of growth. Physical Sciences and Engineering drew the largest share (43%), ahead of Social Sciences and Humanities (29%) and Life Sciences (28%), and women submitted nearly 39% of proposals. For early-career researchers planning a Starting Grant bid, these numbers point to a markedly more crowded field and underline the value of careful preparation.

Interactive publication featuring over 100 success stories and testimonials

The Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions have released an interactive publication collecting more than 100 success stories and testimonials from the programme. For doctoral candidates weighing a fellowship application, it offers concrete examples of the career paths the programme has supported.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 20 October 2026

Science without borders: the lasting legacy of Marie Skłodowska-Curie

Marking 30 years of the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions in 2026, this piece notes that more than 150,000 researchers, including 25 Nobel laureates, have taken part since 1996, with annual support growing from roughly 800 to about 8,000 researchers. It also reports that companies make up almost half of participating organisations, with over 4,000 firms involved in the last five years, many of them small and medium-sized enterprises. For early-career researchers, this highlights the programme's scale and its role in opening routes between academia and industry.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 6 October 2026

Nobel Prizes in Medicine and Physics awarded to former MSCA fellow and supervisor

Two of the 2026 Nobel Prizes went to scientists with ties to the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions: Georg Nagel, a former fellow at the University of Naples Federico II between 1999 and 2000, shared the Medicine prize for work on light-gated ion channels and optogenetics, and Francis Halzen, a supervisor in the ASTRONU neutrino astronomy project, received the Physics prize for his role in IceCube and the detection of astrophysical high-energy neutrinos. The announcement shows how mobility fellowships and training networks can sit within careers that later reach the highest levels of recognition.

European Research Council · 5 October 2026

ERC grantee Peter Hegemann shares Nobel Prize in Medicine

The ERC reports that Peter Hegemann, whose discovery of Channelrhodopsin-1 in 2002 helped found optogenetics, shares the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine with Karl Deisseroth and Georg Nagel. The Council has funded his work since 2016 through an individual Advanced Grant and a jointly held grant with two other researchers, and he joins fifteen earlier ERC grantees who have become Nobel laureates. Statements from the Commissioner and the ERC President stress the importance of investigator-driven, curiosity-led research, a theme relevant to anyone considering where to pursue fundamental questions.

European Research Council · 2 October 2026

Five ERC grantees win 2026 Wolf Prizes

The ERC highlights ERC-funded winners of the 2026 Wolf Prizes. David Kazhdan and Joseph Bernstein received the Mathematics prize for foundational work in representation theory, automorphic forms and algebraic geometry, while David Klenerman and Shankar Balasubramanian shared the Chemistry prize with Pascal Mayer for large-scale, low-cost DNA sequencing methods. The item outlines the Advanced Grant projects behind each laureate, giving early-career researchers a sense of how sustained ERC funding has accompanied long-term programmes of work.
