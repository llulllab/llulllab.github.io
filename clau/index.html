Doctoral and Early-Career Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

These calls trace a path from structured doctoral training through postdoctoral development to running a research programme of your own, under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions and the ERC Consolidator Grant. Read them as a map: each describes a different stage of a research career and what that stage is expected to produce, which helps you decide where your effort belongs over the next cycle.

Of the five calls below, three are applied for by institutions or consortia, with individual positions recruited after the grant is awarded, and two are applied for directly by the individual researcher, together with a host organisation.

ERC Consolidator Grant

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open Closes on 12 January 2027 59 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

The Consolidator Grant funds Principal Investigators who are consolidating their own independent research team or programme. It is aimed at the point where a researcher is building a group and a long-term agenda of their own. The description also allows additional funding for further costs when these are necessary for the proposed work and duly justified in the proposal, so the scale of a project can follow its scientific needs.

Preparation step. Before drafting anything, confirm every eligibility condition on the official topic page. Then write a one-page account of your research programme that shows the independent line of work you have already built and how a consolidated team would carry it forward, noting any costs beyond the standard budget that you would need to justify.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

MSCA COFUND (doctoral programmes)

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 143 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

This call funds doctoral programmes run by participating organisations, so the proposal comes from the institutions, and doctoral candidates are recruited later into the programmes that receive funding. For a prospective doctoral candidate, the outcomes it names are what to expect from such a programme: a broader range of research and professional competences, interdisciplinary and inter-sectoral exposure, and practice in communicating research to peers and the public.

Preparation step

Make a short list of universities and research organisations in your field that run or are building doctoral programmes under this instrument, follow their news and vacancy pages, and be ready for the positions they advertise once a programme is funded; meanwhile, sharpen a project idea and a CV that speak to interdisciplinary training.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

MSCA COFUND (postdoctoral programmes)

MSCA Choose Europe for Science 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 143 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Choose Europe for Science funds postdoctoral programmes at participating organisations, which later recruit researchers into the positions they create. Its stated aims for researchers are concrete career prospects and greater career stability, alongside skills that lead to more autonomy. On the institutional side, it asks organisations to align recruitment and career progression practices with the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment, which shapes the kind of posts these programmes are meant to offer.

Preparation step

Track organisations in your discipline that publicly align with the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment, since these are the ones likely to run such programmes, and watch their career pages for postdoctoral openings after awards are made. In the meantime, build a record that shows growing autonomy, such as leading a paper, a small grant or a teaching component.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-02-01

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027

Opens on 7 April 2027 Closes on 8 September 2027 298 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

Postdoctoral Fellowships fund a researcher's own project carried out with a host organisation, so you write the proposal yourself and choose where to do the work. The outcomes it describes centre on widening your skills and career options through international, inter-sectoral and interdisciplinary experience. Host organisations are also expected to align working conditions with the European Charter for Researchers and to offer solid training and supervision, which is worth weighing when you choose where to go.

Preparation step

Start by checking the mobility rule and all other eligibility conditions on the official topic page, so you do not invest time in a route that does not fit. Once that is settled, identify two or three prospective host groups whose work complements yours, contact a potential supervisor early, and draft an outline that names the new skills and experience the fellowship would give you.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2027

Opens on 26 May 2027 Closes on 23 November 2027 374 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Doctoral Networks fund joint doctoral training delivered by participating organisations working together across sectors and disciplines. Doctoral candidates join through vacancies the networks advertise after funding is awarded. The call expects candidates to gain new research competences, to turn ideas into products and services where relevant, and to strengthen how they communicate with peers and the public, so these positions combine a doctorate with structured cooperation between partners.

Preparation step

Identify research groups and companies in your area that have taken part in doctoral networks before, follow their announcements and the networks they belong to, and prepare to respond when funded networks publish their doctoral positions. Use the waiting time to gain experience that shows you can work across sectors, for example an internship or a collaborative project.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-DN-01-01

PRIVACY STATEMENT for Science is Wonderful! 2027

The Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions have published a privacy statement for the European Commission's Science is Wonderful! 2027 event. It sets out why personal data is collected from participants, how it is handled and protected, and which rights individuals hold over that information. Researchers planning to take part in the event can consult it to understand how their details will be used.

European Research Council · 12 November 2026

Synergy Grants 2026: Examples of projects

The ERC has showcased projects funded in its 2026 call for collaborative team grants, including NAVAR, which studies how migration affects languages learned in early childhood. The team will examine bilingual speakers raised in Germany with Polish or Portuguese as a heritage language who later relocated to Poland or Portugal, and will build an open-access database on how first languages evolve over a lifetime. For early-career linguists and cognitive scientists, the project signals funded interest in bilingualism research with possible relevance to multilingual education and migration policy.

Read the full announcement at European Research Council

European Research Council · 12 November 2026

ERC Synergy Grants 2026: Bringing 48 research teams together to tackle science’s biggest challenges

The ERC has announced 48 funded teams under its 2026 call for grants that bring together two to four researchers with complementary expertise, financed through Horizon Europe. According to the announcement, 17 of these teams include researchers based outside Europe, which the Commission highlights as evidence of the international reach of frontier research. Early-career researchers may find this a useful reference point for how investigator-led, cross-disciplinary collaborations are supported at European level.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 11 November 2026

Developing talents, advancing research : 30 years of EU support to researchers’ skills and career development

A new publication from the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions reviews three decades of EU support for researcher training and career development. It describes the programme as the EU's main research and training scheme, with a budget of 6.6 billion euros for 2021 to 2027, and notes that it has backed more than 150,000 researchers, among them 25 Nobel laureates. For doctoral and postdoctoral researchers, it offers context on a key funding route that emphasises skills, mobility and career progression across all disciplines.

Read the full announcement at Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions

European Research Council · 5 November 2026

Commission invites nominations for the next President of the European Research Council

The European Commission has opened nominations for the next President of the European Research Council, with research organisations across the EU invited to put forward candidates in November 2026. The successor to Professor Maria Leptin will begin in autumn 2027 for a four-year term that may be renewed once. The appointment matters to early-career researchers because the ERC President helps shape one of Europe's most prominent funders of investigator-led research.

Read the full announcement at European Research Council

European Research Council · 23 October 2026

Starting Grants 2027 applications: Facts and figures

Preliminary figures for the ERC Starting Grants 2027 call show 7,710 applications, a rise of 60.4% on the previous call, following earlier increases of 22.4% and 13% in the 2026 and 2025 calls. Physical Sciences and Engineering made up 43% of proposals, Social Sciences and Humanities 29% and Life Sciences 28%, while women submitted nearly 39% of applications. Early-career researchers considering this grant should expect a substantially more competitive field than in past rounds.
