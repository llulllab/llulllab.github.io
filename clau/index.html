Doctoral funding radar · 24 November 2026

Doctoral and Early-Career Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

Planning a next application is easier when you understand what each funding instrument is designed to achieve. The calls below come from the European Research Council and the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, and they cover several stages of a research career, from structured doctoral training to leading a research programme of your own. Read each summary against your current position, then use the suggested step to decide where your preparation time is best spent.

Of the five calls below, three are applied for by institutions or consortia, with individual positions recruited after the grant is awarded, and two are applied for directly by the individual researcher, together with a host organisation.

ERC Consolidator Grant

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open Closes on 12 January 2027 49 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

The Consolidator Grant supports Principal Investigators who are consolidating their own independent research team or programme. It funds a research agenda you lead, not a position inside someone else's project. The description expects that a Principal Investigator has already shown evidence of research independence, so the call suits someone whose work already has a recognisable direction of its own. It also allows additional funding for further costs, provided the request is properly justified in the proposal.

Preparation step. Write a one-page account of the independent programme you are building: the question your team pursues, which results are clearly yours, and what a five-year horizon would let you consolidate. Before going further, check every eligibility condition on the official topic page. If your plan involves costs beyond the standard grant, note early which ones they are and why the work cannot proceed without them, since the description requires that justification.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

MSCA COFUND (doctoral programmes)

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 133 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

This Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND call finances doctoral programmes, and the proposals come from organisations, not from individual candidates. For doctoral candidates, the stated outcomes include a broader range of research and transferable competences, along with experience that crosses borders, sectors and disciplines. The programmes it funds will open doctoral positions only after an award, usually well after the institutional deadline.

Preparation step

Make a shortlist of universities and research organisations that run or co-finance structured doctoral programmes in your field, and follow their careers pages and newsletters so you see the positions they advertise if they are funded. In the meantime, build up your communication record, for example a public talk or an accessible write-up of your thesis work, because the call explicitly values engagement with the general public.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

MSCA COFUND

MSCA Choose Europe for Science 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 133 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Choose Europe for Science is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND call where participating organisations design programmes for researchers, so funding goes to the organisations and not to individuals. For the researchers later supported, the description points to concrete career prospects, greater career stability and growing autonomy. On the organisational side, it asks for recruitment and progression practices aligned with the European Charter for Researchers, the Council Recommendation on attracting and retaining talent, and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment.

Preparation step

Identify organisations in your field that publicly commit to the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment, and keep an eye on the vacancy pages of those likely to run such a programme so you can respond quickly once positions appear. Prepare a CV that presents more than publications, including teaching, mentoring and openly shared outputs, since the reform agenda the call cites favours a broader view of research contributions.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-02-01

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027

Opens on 7 April 2027 Closes on 8 September 2027 288 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

Postdoctoral Fellowships let you propose your own research project and carry it out with a host organisation that you choose and approach yourself. The expected outcomes stress a wider set of research and professional skills and a perspective shaped by international, cross-sector and interdisciplinary work. Participating organisations are also expected to offer working conditions in line with the European Charter for Researchers and to provide good-quality supervision.

Preparation step

Draft a two-page outline of the project you would want to lead. Then contact a prospective supervisor at a host organisation early, and ask how they would support your training and supervision. Before investing serious time, confirm the mobility rule and all other eligibility conditions on the official topic page, because your choice of host depends on them.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2027

Opens on 26 May 2027 Closes on 23 November 2027 364 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Doctoral Networks fund partnerships of organisations that train doctoral candidates together, and the proposals are prepared at that level, not by the candidates. For the people who join later, the description highlights new research competences, better career options inside and outside universities, and, where relevant, knowledge that helps turn ideas into products and services. It also emphasises cooperation and exchange of knowledge between sectors and disciplines among the partners.

Preparation step

Find research groups in your area that have taken part in Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks before, follow their announcements, and watch for the doctoral positions they advertise once a network is funded. To be a strong candidate later, look for experience outside academia, such as an industry placement, a collaboration with a company or a project with a practical application, since the call stresses moving knowledge between sectors.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-DN-01-01

European Research Council · 13 November 2026

Three new ERC Scientific Council members and two new vice-presidents

The European Research Council has named three new members of its Scientific Council, who will serve an initial four-year term starting in early 2027, and has extended the terms of five existing members by two years. Two members were also elected vice-presidents: Thomas Henzinger for the physical sciences and engineering, and Luke O'Neill for the life sciences. Because this body governs the ERC, its membership shapes the funding schemes that many early-career researchers aim for.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 13 November 2026

PRIVACY STATEMENT for Science is Wonderful! 2027

The Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions have published a privacy statement for the 2027 edition of Science is Wonderful!, an event organised by the European Commission. The document explains why and how participants' personal data will be collected, used and protected, and what rights individuals have over that information. Researchers who plan to take part should read it before registering or sharing their details.

European Research Council · 12 November 2026

Synergy Grants 2026: Examples of projects

The ERC has profiled several projects funded under its 2026 team grant round. One of them, NAVAR, will study how migration affects languages learned in early childhood, focusing on bilingual speakers raised in Germany with Polish or Portuguese who later relocated to Poland or Portugal. The project plans an open-access database, a resource that could interest early-career linguists and researchers working on bilingualism, cognition or migration.

Read the full announcement at European Research Council

European Research Council · 12 November 2026

ERC Synergy Grants 2026: Bringing 48 research teams together to tackle science’s biggest challenges

The European Research Council has funded 48 teams in its 2026 round of grants for small groups of two to four researchers who combine complementary expertise on questions none could address alone. Funding comes through Horizon Europe, and 17 of the selected teams include researchers based outside Europe. For early-career researchers, the results show how collaborative, investigator-led projects with international partners can be supported at this scale.

Read the full announcement at European Research Council

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 11 November 2026

Developing talents, advancing research : 30 years of EU support to researchers’ skills and career development

This Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions publication reviews three decades of EU support for researchers' training, skills and career progression. It describes the programme as the EU's main research and training scheme, with a budget of €6.6 billion for 2021 to 2027 and more than 150,000 researchers supported, including 25 Nobel laureates. For doctoral and postdoctoral researchers considering mobility fellowships or doctoral networks, it gives useful context on the programme's scope and priorities.

Read the full announcement at Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions

European Research Council · 5 November 2026

Commission invites nominations for the next President of the European Research Council

The European Commission has asked research organisations across the EU to nominate candidates for the next President of the European Research Council, with a deadline in late November 2026. The successor to Maria Leptin will take office in autumn 2027 for a four-year mandate that can be renewed once. The appointment matters to early-career researchers because the President helps set the direction of a major source of frontier research funding in Europe.
