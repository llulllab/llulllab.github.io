Doctoral funding radar · 30 October 2026

Doctoral and Early-Career Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

This batch of calls spans instruments that fund doctoral training, postdoctoral research, and independent investigator programmes under the European research framework. Whether you are scoping out where structured doctoral positions may emerge or preparing a fellowship proposal of your own, each call rewards careful, early groundwork and a clear research vision.

Of the five calls below, three are applied for by institutions or consortia, with individual positions recruited after the grant is awarded, and two are applied for directly by the individual researcher, together with a host organisation.

ERC Consolidator Grant

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open Closes on 12 January 2027 74 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

The ERC Consolidator Grant targets principal investigators who are building on an established track record of independent research. For early-career researchers who have moved past the postdoctoral stage and are shaping their own team or programme, this instrument offers substantial funding over a five-year period, with the possibility of requesting additional resources when justified by the proposed work.

Preparation step. Draft a concise case for why your research programme requires the scale and duration this grant provides, and identify which cost categories might warrant a request for additional funding; ground that justification in concrete methodological or infrastructural needs rather than generic ambitions.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

MSCA COFUND (doctoral programmes)

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 158 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND for doctoral programmes channels co-financing to organisations that run structured doctoral training. These programmes are designed to deepen candidates' research competences while broadening their transferable skills through international, inter-sectoral, and interdisciplinary exposure. For doctoral candidates, the positions that eventually result from funded projects can offer a rich training environment with enhanced networking opportunities.

Preparation step

Identify research organisations and universities that have previously coordinated or participated in Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND doctoral programmes, bookmark their vacancy pages, and begin strengthening the parts of your profile (publications, interdisciplinary experience, outreach) that these programmes typically value when they later advertise recruited positions.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

MSCA COFUND (postdoctoral programmes)

MSCA Choose Europe for Science 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 158 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

This Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND call focuses on postdoctoral programmes under the Choose Europe for Science strand, aiming to improve career stability and autonomy for researchers while aligning participating organisations with the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment. For postdoctoral researchers, funded programmes under this call can offer structured career development within institutions committed to transparent recruitment and progression practices.

Preparation step

Start tracking organisations that have a history with this instrument or have publicly committed to the European Charter for Researchers, since these are likely candidates to coordinate future programmes; when such programmes are awarded and advertise openings, a tailored application that speaks to career autonomy and alignment with reformed assessment principles will be well positioned.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-02-01

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027

Opens on 7 April 2027 Closes on 8 September 2027 313 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowships let a researcher propose an individual project carried out at a host organisation, with the explicit goal of expanding both research expertise and transferable competences. The call emphasises international, inter-sectoral, and interdisciplinary dimensions, making it well suited for postdoctoral researchers who want to pivot into a new environment or deepen a cross-disciplinary line of inquiry.

Preparation step

Before investing significant preparation time, verify the mobility rule and all eligibility conditions on the official topic page; then approach a prospective supervisor at a host organisation whose expertise complements your proposed project, and co-develop a training and research plan that credibly integrates the international or inter-sectoral dimension the call highlights.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2027

Opens on 26 May 2027 Closes on 23 November 2027 389 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks fund consortia of organisations that jointly design and deliver doctoral training, with an emphasis on cooperation across sectors and disciplines. The resulting projects create funded doctoral positions that expose candidates to multiple institutional environments, equipping them with skills valued both inside and outside academia.

Preparation step

Monitor research groups and industry partners that have previously participated in Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks, especially those whose thematic focus aligns with your interests; once new networks are awarded, they will publish open calls for doctoral candidates, and having a clear research profile and evidence of cross-disciplinary curiosity will help you stand out in those recruitment rounds.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-DN-01-01

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 20 October 2026

Science without borders: the lasting legacy of Marie Skłodowska-Curie

An article published via Horizon Magazine traces how the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, inspired by Marie Skłodowska-Curie's vision of borderless science, has supported over 150,000 researchers since its creation in 1996, including 25 Nobel laureates. The programme has grown from funding around 800 researchers per year to roughly 8,000 annually, with companies making up nearly half of participating organisations. For doctoral and postdoctoral researchers, the piece illustrates the programme's scale and its strong links to both academic and industry sectors.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 6 October 2026

Nobel Prizes in Medicine and Physics awarded to former MSCA fellow and supervisor

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was awarded to Georg Nagel, a former Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions fellow who studied the role of the Na+ Ca2+ exchanger in neurodegeneration at the University of Naples Federico II, sharing the prize for discoveries in optogenetics. The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics went to Francis Halzen, who supervised the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions funded ASTRONU project on neutrino astronomy and the development of large-scale neutrino telescopes. These awards underscore how early-career funding through the programme can be part of trajectories leading to the highest levels of scientific recognition.

Read the full announcement at Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 30 September 2026

Over €210 million boost for the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions to support more researchers in Europe

The European Commission has announced an additional EUR 210 million for the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, directed toward Postdoctoral Fellowships and Doctoral Networks, as part of its broader effort to attract and retain research talent in Europe. The boost responds to record demand: the 2026 Postdoctoral Fellowships call received 21,627 applications, the highest number ever for any single funding action across more than 40 years of EU framework programmes. With over EUR 1.3 billion foreseen for the programme in 2027, this increase should translate into more funded positions for doctoral and postdoctoral researchers in the final calls under Horizon Europe.

Read the full announcement at Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 25 September 2026

Cancer research in the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions

The Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions has published a document highlighting cancer research funded through the programme, which has a total budget of EUR 6.6 billion for 2021 to 2027. The publication illustrates how the programme's bottom-up, excellence-driven approach supports researchers working on cancer at all career stages, making it relevant for doctoral candidates and postdoctoral researchers seeking funding in this field.
