Doctoral funding radar · 11 November 2026

Doctoral and Early-Career Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

Planning a next funding move is easier when you can see the whole pipeline at once, from structured doctoral training to grants that sustain a research programme you already run. The calls below span that range under the ERC and the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, and each rewards a different kind of groundwork. Read them as a map of where your profile could fit, both on the next cycle and further ahead.

Of the five calls below, three are applied for by institutions or consortia, with individual positions recruited after the grant is awarded, and two are applied for directly by the individual researcher, together with a host organisation.

ERC Consolidator Grant

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open Closes on 12 January 2027 62 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

The Consolidator Grant funds Principal Investigators who are consolidating their own independent research team or programme. It is a grant for building on work you already lead, not for starting from scratch. The description also allows additional funding for further costs when these are necessary to carry out the proposed work, provided the request is duly justified in the proposal.

Preparation step. Write a one-page outline of the research programme you intend to consolidate, mark any element that would need costs beyond the standard grant and note why it is necessary, then verify all eligibility conditions on the official topic page and open an early conversation with a prospective host organisation.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

MSCA COFUND (doctoral programmes)

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 146 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

This Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND call lets organisations seek co-funding for doctoral programmes, so the proposal comes from the organisations and doctoral candidates join funded programmes later through open vacancies. For a prospective doctoral applicant, it signals where structured training may appear. The description promises supported candidates a broader set of research and transferable competences, plus contact with peers and with the general public.

Preparation step

Make a shortlist of graduate schools and universities that have run doctoral programmes under this instrument before, follow their vacancy pages and newsletters, and watch for the positions they advertise once a programme is funded, which can be a year or more after the institutional deadline.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

MSCA COFUND (postdoctoral programmes)

MSCA Choose Europe for Science 2027

Opens on 8 December 2026 Closes on 6 April 2027 146 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Choose Europe for Science co-funds postdoctoral programmes run by participating organisations, and researchers are hired into them after the grant is awarded. Its expected outcomes focus on concrete career prospects and greater career stability, along with a wider range of academic and research competences that build autonomy. The organisations are expected to align recruitment and progression practices with the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment.

Preparation step

Identify research organisations in your field that publicly commit to the European Charter for Researchers and to reformed research assessment, follow their career pages for postdoctoral programmes advertised once funded, and use the waiting period to strengthen your publication and teaching record.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-02-01

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027

Opens on 7 April 2027 Closes on 8 September 2027 301 days left

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

Postdoctoral Fellowships under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions are applied for by the researcher together with a host organisation, so you shape the project yourself. The description stresses new approaches to research forged through international, inter-sectoral and interdisciplinary experience. It also names the quality of training and supervision on the host side, so the fit with a supervisor matters as much as the idea.

Preparation step

Before drafting anything substantial, check the mobility rule and every other eligibility condition on the official topic page; once that is settled, approach a prospective supervisor with a short project sketch and ask how their group would support your training and outreach.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2027

Opens on 26 May 2027 Closes on 23 November 2027 377 days left

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Doctoral Networks are proposed by partnerships of organisations across sectors and disciplines, and the doctoral candidates are hired after funding is awarded through separate open calls. For someone considering a PhD, the networks offer training spread across several partners. The description mentions new knowledge that can turn ideas into products and services where relevant, alongside stronger cooperation between sectors.

Preparation step

Follow research groups and companies in your area that take part in Doctoral Networks, set up alerts for the doctoral vacancies such networks publish once funded, and build a CV that shows genuine interest in work across academic and non-academic settings.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-DN-01-01

European Research Council · 5 November 2026

Commission invites nominations for the next President of the European Research Council

The European Commission is asking research organisations across the EU to put forward candidates to lead the European Research Council, with nominations due in late November 2026. The appointee will begin in autumn 2027, taking over from Professor Maria Leptin for a four-year term that can be renewed once. Early-career researchers who may one day seek ERC funding have a stake in who guides the council's direction.

European Research Council · 23 October 2026

Starting Grants 2027 applications: Facts and figures

Preliminary figures for the ERC Starting Grants 2027 call, which closed in October 2026, show 7,710 proposals: a jump of 60.4% over the previous round, after rises of 22.4% and 13% in the two calls before it. Physical Sciences and Engineering made up 43% of submissions, Social Sciences and Humanities 29% and Life Sciences 28%, while women submitted nearly 39% of proposals. For early-career researchers considering this scheme, the numbers point to a sharply more competitive field.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 20 October 2026

Science without borders: the lasting legacy of Marie Skłodowska-Curie

Marking 30 years of the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions in 2026, this article notes that over 150,000 researchers, including 25 Nobel laureates, have taken part since 1996, and that annual support has grown from about 800 to roughly 8,000 researchers. Companies represent almost half of participating organisations, and more than 4,000 firms have joined projects over the last five years. For early-career researchers, this highlights how the programme connects academic training with careers in industry and other sectors.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions · 6 October 2026

Nobel Prizes in Medicine and Physics awarded to former MSCA fellow and supervisor

Two Nobel laureates of 2026 have ties to the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions. Georg Nagel, who shared the Medicine prize for work on light-gated ion channels and optogenetics, held a fellowship at the University of Naples Federico II between 1999 and 2000, and Francis Halzen, awarded in Physics for contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory, supervised within the MSCA-funded ASTRONU project on neutrino astronomy. These examples show that mobility fellowships and training networks can sit early in careers that later reach the highest levels of recognition.

European Research Council · 5 October 2026

ERC grantee Peter Hegemann shares Nobel Prize in Medicine

The ERC reports that Peter Hegemann, a co-recipient of the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, is the sixteenth ERC grantee to receive a Nobel Prize, having held an Advanced Grant and a second, jointly held grant with two other researchers since 2016. His discovery of Channelrhodopsin-1 in 2002 helped establish optogenetics, a method for controlling cell activity with light. The ERC President used the occasion to argue for continued European investment in curiosity-driven research, the kind of research ERC grants are designed to support.
