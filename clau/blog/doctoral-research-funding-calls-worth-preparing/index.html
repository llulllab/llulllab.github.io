Doctoral and Early-Career Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Clau team. As of 30 October 2026.

This set of calls spans instruments relevant to researchers at different career stages, from those considering doctoral training through to those consolidating an independent research programme. Whether you are shaping a research vision or scanning the horizon for structured training opportunities, each call rewards careful preparation well ahead of the deadline.

Of the five calls below, three are applied for by institutions or consortia, with individual positions recruited after the grant is awarded, and two are applied for directly by the individual researcher, together with a host organisation.

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. ERC Consolidator Grant.

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

The ERC Consolidator Grant targets Principal Investigators who are building on demonstrated research independence and consolidating their own team or programme. It offers funding for up to 60 months, with the possibility of requesting additional funding when justified by the proposed work. For early-career researchers approaching this stage, it represents a significant opportunity to secure resources for an ambitious, self-directed research agenda.

Preparation step. Draft a concise argument for why your proposed project requires resources beyond the standard grant ceiling, since the call allows additional funding requests that must be duly justified in the proposal; even if you ultimately stay within the base amount, working through this exercise will sharpen your sense of scope and feasibility.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens 8 December 2026, closes 6 April 2027. MSCA COFUND (doctoral programmes).

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

This Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND call funds doctoral programmes run by organisations that commit to high-quality, internationally oriented research training. Doctoral candidates do not apply to this call themselves; instead, organisations design and propose structured programmes, and successful ones later advertise funded positions openly. The emphasis on interdisciplinary and inter-sectoral experience signals that the resulting positions will offer training environments reaching well beyond a single lab or department.

Preparation step. Identify research groups and universities that have previously coordinated or participated in Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND doctoral programmes, bookmark their vacancy pages, and begin building a relationship with potential supervisors so you are well positioned when funded positions are advertised after grants are awarded.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

MSCA Choose Europe for Science 2027

Opens 8 December 2026, closes 6 April 2027. MSCA COFUND (postdoctoral programmes).

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

This Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND call, branded Choose Europe for Science, supports postdoctoral programmes designed by organisations to offer career stability and structured professional development. It places particular weight on alignment with the European Charter for Researchers and the 2023 Council Recommendation on attracting and retaining research talent. Researchers benefit not by applying here directly but by later joining the programmes that funded organisations create.

Preparation step. Keep a running list of organisations known to participate in this instrument and monitor their human-resources or research-office announcements, since funded programmes will recruit postdoctoral researchers through open calls that typically appear months after the grant is signed.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-02-01

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027

Opens 7 April 2027, closes 8 September 2027. MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships.

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowships let a researcher propose a self-designed project carried out at a chosen host organisation, with the goal of deepening both research expertise and a broader set of professional competences. The call values international, inter-sectoral, and interdisciplinary dimensions, meaning a compelling proposal will articulate how the fellowship environment differs meaningfully from the candidate's prior experience.

Preparation step. Before investing significant time, verify the mobility rule and all eligibility conditions on the official topic page; then approach a prospective supervisor at your intended host to co-develop a project outline that clearly shows how the placement will expand your methodological or sectoral range beyond what you have done so far.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2027

Opens 26 May 2027, closes 23 November 2027. MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks fund multi-partner consortia that train doctoral candidates through jointly supervised, cross-sectoral research projects. Because the call is addressed to consortia of organisations rather than to individual candidates, the doctoral positions it generates only become available once a network has been selected and the grant agreement signed. The description highlights improved cooperation between sectors and disciplines, so the resulting positions tend to involve secondments and exposure to non-academic partners.

Preparation step. Map consortia and research groups that have coordinated or partnered in previous rounds of this instrument, reach out to principal investigators whose research aligns with your interests, and set alerts on their project websites so you can respond quickly when they publish recruitment calls for funded doctoral positions.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-DN-01-01
