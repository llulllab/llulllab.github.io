Doctoral and Early-Career Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Clau team. As of 25 September 2026.

The calls listed here span Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions instruments, an ERC Consolidator Grant, and related doctoral and postdoctoral programmes, with deadlines ranging from the near term to 2027. Some are institutional opportunities whose funded positions will be advertised later, while one allows individual researchers to apply directly, so understanding who submits what is essential before investing preparation time.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks bring together universities, research institutes, and non-academic partners to create structured doctoral training programmes across disciplines and sectors. When funded, these networks advertise open doctoral positions that candidates can apply for through competitive recruitment processes. Following active research groups that participate in such consortia puts you in a strong position when those vacancy calls appear. The expected outcomes described for doctoral candidates include expanded research skills, networking capacity, and pathways into careers both within and beyond academia.

Preparation step. Identify research groups and institutions in your field whose work aligns with the training themes described in this instrument, note which ones have previously participated in Doctoral Networks, and build a relationship with potential supervisors so your profile is visible when funded positions are advertised after the grant is awarded.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. ERC Consolidator Grant.

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

The ERC Consolidator Grant supports principal investigators who are consolidating an independent research programme and have demonstrated research independence, making it relevant for early-career researchers who are approaching or have reached that stage. Understanding the grant's scope and scale helps you plan the trajectory of your own research profile and publication record toward a competitive application in the future. The grant covers a substantial period of research activity, giving awardees the runway to build a team and deepen a programme of work. Tracking this call also helps doctoral candidates identify group leaders who may be recruiting into ERC-funded teams.

Preparation step. Map your current record of research independence against what the call describes as expected of a Consolidator Grant principal investigator, then identify concrete milestones in publications, supervision, and grant leadership that would strengthen a future application at this career stage.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens 8 December 2026, closes 6 April 2027. MSCA COFUND (doctoral programmes).

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions COFUND for doctoral programmes co-funds institutional schemes that offer doctoral training with an international, inter-sectoral, and interdisciplinary character. Institutions apply for this funding to establish or expand their own doctoral programmes, and individual candidates are recruited into those programmes after the grant is awarded. The described outcomes for doctoral candidates emphasise a broad and diverse skill set alongside enhanced communication with both scientific peers and the wider public. Keeping an eye on which institutions are active in this instrument means you will be among the first to see the doctoral positions they open once funded.

Preparation step. Research universities and research organisations in your area of interest that have previously run COFUND doctoral programmes, follow their graduate school communications and vacancy pages, and prepare a concise research statement so you can respond quickly when positions are advertised.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

MSCA Choose Europe for Science 2027

Opens 8 December 2026, closes 6 April 2027. MSCA COFUND (postdoctoral programmes).

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions Choose Europe for Science is a COFUND instrument aimed at postdoctoral programmes, and it is institutions rather than individual researchers that submit proposals. The call's described outcomes for supported researchers include concrete career prospects, increased career stability, and a broader set of academic and transferable competences developed through the programme. Notably, the call references alignment with the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment, signalling that participating organisations commit to structured career support frameworks. Researchers who position themselves within institutions active in this instrument stand to benefit from those structured conditions once funded positions open.

Preparation step. Look into which research organisations in your field are working toward alignment with the European Charter for Researchers and related frameworks mentioned in the call description, as these are the institutions most likely to apply for this instrument and subsequently advertise postdoctoral positions under it.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-02-01

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027

Opens 7 April 2027, closes 8 September 2027. MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships.

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowships are one of the few instruments in this set where the individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation and supervisor. The call describes outcomes that include an expanded skill set, new approaches to research and innovation forged through international and inter-sectoral experience, and stronger communication capacity with scientific and public audiences. This makes it a meaningful option for postdoctoral researchers who want to design a project around their own research agenda while gaining experience in a new environment. Because the proposal is developed jointly with a host, the quality of the relationship with your prospective supervisor is central to a strong application.

Preparation step. Begin identifying prospective host organisations and supervisors whose research agenda complements your own, open an early conversation about a possible joint project, and verify the mobility rule and all eligibility conditions on the official topic page before committing significant preparation time.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01
