Doctoral and Early-Career Research Funding Calls Worth Preparing For

By the Clau team. As of 29 September 2026.

European research funding cycles reward those who plan well in advance, and the calls gathered here span doctoral training, postdoctoral development, and independent research consolidation. Knowing which instrument fits which career stage, and what each call is actually designed to achieve, is the first step toward making a well-timed, well-grounded move.

Of the five calls below, three are applied for by institutions or consortia, with individual positions recruited after the grant is awarded, and two are applied for directly by the individual researcher, together with a host organisation.

MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026

Open, closes 24 November 2026. MSCA Doctoral Networks.

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Doctoral Networks bring together universities, research institutes, and non-academic partners to design and deliver structured doctoral training across sectors and disciplines. The programme is explicitly oriented toward building research and transferable skills that open career paths both inside and outside academia. For a prospective doctoral candidate, the networks funded through this call represent some of the most internationally connected training environments available in Europe. Understanding which research communities are active in this instrument helps you recognise where strong, well-resourced doctoral positions are likely to emerge.

Preparation step. Map the research groups, departments, and non-academic partners in your field that have previously coordinated or participated in Doctoral Networks proposals; follow their news channels and vacancy pages so you are well positioned to respond when they advertise the doctoral roles that a funded network will open.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01

ERC Consolidator Grant

Open, closes 12 January 2027. ERC Consolidator Grant.

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

The ERC Consolidator Grant is designed for researchers who have already demonstrated independence and are building or consolidating their own research team or programme. The call description makes clear that this is a substantial, multi-year award intended to give a Principal Investigator the resources to pursue an ambitious, self-defined research agenda. For an early-career researcher approaching this stage, it represents a structured opportunity to establish a long-term line of inquiry under your own scientific leadership. The scale of support described in the call reflects the expectation that funded projects will produce significant, original contributions to their field.

Preparation step. Draft a concise outline of the independent research programme you would propose, articulating the scientific case for your specific questions and identifying a host institution whose environment would genuinely support that work; open a conversation with potential hosts well before the submission window.

Official topic page: ERC-2027-COG

MSCA COFUND 2027

Opens 8 December 2026, closes 6 April 2027. MSCA COFUND (doctoral programmes).

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions COFUND for doctoral programmes co-funds existing or new doctoral schemes at participating organisations, with the explicit goal of enhancing the quality, sustainability, and international character of those programmes. The call description emphasises interdisciplinary and inter-sectoral experience as core features of what funded candidates will gain. For a prospective doctoral candidate, COFUND-backed programmes tend to embed structured international and cross-sector exposure directly into the training design. Tracking which institutions are developing COFUND proposals in your area gives you advance notice of where well-structured, internationally oriented doctoral positions will appear.

Preparation step. Identify universities and research institutes in your field that emphasise interdisciplinary or inter-sectoral doctoral training, and engage with their graduate schools or research offices to understand their funding pipeline, so your profile is visible when recruited positions are advertised after a grant is awarded.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-01-01

MSCA Choose Europe for Science 2027

Opens 8 December 2026, closes 6 April 2027. MSCA COFUND (postdoctoral programmes).

Who applies. Institutions or consortia apply. Individual positions are recruited openly after the grant is awarded.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Choose Europe for Science is a COFUND instrument specifically for postdoctoral programmes, and its description places notable emphasis on career stability, greater researcher autonomy, and alignment with the European Charter for Researchers and the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment. This signals that funded programmes are expected to take working conditions and career progression seriously, not just research output. The explicit mention of attracting and retaining research talent in Europe gives this call a particular relevance for postdoctoral researchers considering their long-term base. Positions recruited through funded programmes are likely to sit within organisations that have made concrete commitments to researcher-friendly practices.

Preparation step. Research which organisations in your discipline have engaged with the European Charter for Researchers or the Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment, as these are the kinds of institutions likely to build competitive proposals under this call; follow their postdoctoral recruitment channels for the vacancy announcements that will follow if they are funded.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-COFUND-02-01

MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2027

Opens 7 April 2027, closes 8 September 2027. MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships.

Who applies. The individual researcher applies directly, together with a chosen host organisation.

Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Postdoctoral Fellowships support individual postdoctoral researchers in designing and carrying out a project at a host organisation, with the call description pointing to gains in research and transferable skills, broader professional networks, and experience across sectors and disciplines. The fellowship is framed around the fellow's own development and scientific agenda, making it one of the more researcher-led instruments described here. The description also notes expected benefits for host organisations in terms of alignment with the European Charter for Researchers, which suggests hosts take their supervisory and working-condition commitments seriously. Preparing a strong application means having a clear research idea and a host whose expertise genuinely complements yours.

Preparation step. Before investing significant time in a draft, verify the mobility rule and all eligibility conditions on the official topic page; then begin developing your research proposal concept and reach out to prospective host researchers whose work connects directly with your planned project, since early dialogue with a host shapes both the scientific case and the feasibility of the proposal.

Official topic page: HORIZON-MSCA-2027-PF-01-01
