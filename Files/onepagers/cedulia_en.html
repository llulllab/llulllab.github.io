The Problem

Producing even a few seconds of usable film means a shoot, a crew, or a long post-production chain. Independent producers, small studios, and brand teams cannot justify that for every idea they want to see. Existing tools return a draft, a storyboard, or a silent animation. There is no service that takes a written description and a reference photograph and returns a finished, sounded shot.

How It Works
01
Describe
Describe the shot in plain language: setting, action, mood, and the line to be spoken. Optionally attach a reference photograph to guide the look.
02
Produce
The studio produces one continuous cinematic take, with dialogue and ambient sound, in your chosen aspect ratio.
03
Deliver
The result is checked before delivery, then the finished film is presented for download. Films are available for up to 24 hours, not archived.
What Sets It Apart
  • 01A finished shot, not a storyboard. Picture and sound are produced together in one continuous cinematic take.
  • 02Reference-guided. A supplied photograph guides the look of the shot.
  • 03Honest working surface. The interface shows the stage, the elapsed time, and what a film will cost in credits before you commit it.
