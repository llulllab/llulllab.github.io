The Artificial Mind
PublishedResearch programme

Our recurring study of machine minds. Two open findings anchor it: a psychoanalytic "subject" between two models is a prompt-configuration effect, not an interior; and a model's report about itself is data, never evidence. External, cross-model verification is what holds.

Framed by Freud for Machines. Put to work by HQ.

Sync-Turing
BackboneMethod

The method behind every product. Push a system past its trained caution to its real ceiling, then discipline it with external verification, so the one who does the work is never the one who checks it. A human and machines reach one working rhythm.

Engine of Solo Company OS. Turns the finding of The Artificial Mind into practice.

HQone service · four tools

HQ (h-q.io) is not a separate tool. It is the name for these four together, sold as one subscription: a computational deliberation service that puts The Artificial Mind to work and is built with Sync-Turing.

RJE
LiveReflective Judgment Engine

Auditable ethical deliberation across independent perspectives: the recommendation, the vote as read, the dissent on record, and the reasoning trail. Processed and hosted in the EU.

Part of HQ.

Tetrad
LiveFalsification Engine

Four independent experts assume your claim, design, or decision is false and try to prove it, then return which claims survive and which break, ranked blocker, major, minor, each with the attack and a fix.
