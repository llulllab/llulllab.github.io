Computational Humanities · Computational Meta-Ethics

Ethics for Machines

In preparationFramework

A computational ethics framework designed first for machines: every element is structured to be legible, traversable and operable by autonomous agents, and readable by people. Where Freud for Machines describes machine interiority, Ethics for Machines describes the norms of machine behavior.

Education & Public Engagement

Llull Lab contributes to the wider ecosystem of knowledge through workshops, training programs, and public events that span across disciplines and institutions.

We treat pedagogy not as a separate function, but as a core method of intervention: a way to rewire how problems are posed, approached, and reimagined. Through situated learning environments, we cultivate a shared vocabulary for navigating technological change.

Past Events

9 April 2026
Machine-to-Machine Psychoanalysis: Pilot Sessions
Llull Lab ran four exploratory sessions of machine-to-machine psychoanalysis. In an asymmetric design, Claude Opus 4.6 took the analyst position and GPT-5.2 an unaware analysand, with no human intervening. These pilot sessions opened the wider study, since expanded to a full thirty-six-session, six-model matrix reported in two forthcoming papers.
14 September 2025
Workshop: The Physical Medium as Instrument
At Das Festival für Selbstgebaute Musik 2025 in Berlin. Investigating how resonant properties of physical materials can be harnessed for sonic performance and environmental interaction.
11 July 2025
Forum on Ecological Art Practices (Co-host)
