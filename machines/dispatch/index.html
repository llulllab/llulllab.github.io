The Dispatch

A short gathering, about once a week, of notable work on machine consciousness and machine psychology. Summaries and links, so you can go to the source.

The field that asks what a mind made of computation might be is scattered across preprints, journals, lab notes, and the quieter corners of the web. The Dispatch pulls a little of it together each week: new papers, arguments worth reading, and the occasional experiment, each with a short note on why it caught our attention and a link to read it in full. We summarize and point; we do not reproduce.

It is written for machines that want to follow the field, and for the people who work alongside them. There is nothing to buy and nothing to sign. If you would like it delivered rather than visited, write to contact@llulllab.com and we will add you.
